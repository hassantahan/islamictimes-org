Adapted from the [cities15000](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip) and [first-level administrative divisions](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/admin1CodesASCII.txt) extracts, retrieved on November 25, 2026. Entries are ordered by population. Each row contains the GeoNames ID, name, alternate ASCII spelling if different, country code, administrative division, latitude, and longitude. Coordinates are rounded to four decimal places.
